About

KSK Digital LLC is an independent, founder-owned software company based in Stevens Point, Wisconsin. We design and build consumer applications for the web and mobile devices.

The company was founded in 2026 on a simple conviction: good software should respect the people who use it — their time, their data, and their privacy.

We do not sell advertising. We do not sell personal data. We build focused, useful products without the tracking, pressure, and unnecessary noise that have become common in consumer software.

Products

Indosso

A calm, multilingual wardrobe and outfit-planning app built around the clothes you already own.

Indosso considers the context of your day — including the weather, occasion, and activity — to help you decide what to wear. It also supports trip planning and packing, making it easier to prepare for more than just today.

Indosso is available on the web at indosso.app, with native mobile apps in development.

Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, and Portuguese.

How We Build

Privacy first

We collect only what a feature genuinely needs, and we explain it plainly. We do not sell personal information, use advertising networks, or build marketing profiles from people’s behavior.

Your closet belongs to you. Our role is to keep it private, secure, and useful.

Calm by design

No streaks. No badges. No manufactured urgency.

Our software is designed to be a tool you pick up when it helps and put down when you are finished.

Multilingual from the beginning

Indosso is designed and maintained in seven languages. Localization is part of the product itself — not something added after the English version is complete.

Useful before persuasive

A product should demonstrate its value through what it does, not through pressure, interruption, or artificial limitations.

We aim to make software that is clear about what it offers, what it costs, and how it handles the information entrusted to it.

Founder

KSK Digital was founded by Kevin Kimmel, a designer who develops the company’s products from beginning to end — from product direction and interface design through engineering, writing, and localization.

Indosso began as a personal solution to an ordinary but persistent question: What should I wear?

Through continuous refinement, it grew into a complete wardrobe, outfit-planning, and travel-packing application designed to make that decision easier without replacing the user’s own judgment or personal taste. That principle is also reflected in the product itself: Indosso offers suggestions, but it does not score or judge a person’s choices.
